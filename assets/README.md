# Asset dell’invito

## Decorazioni

`images/invitation-scene.jpg` è uno sfondo originale generato con lo strumento
integrato ImageGen: satin nero, rose blu, lampadario oro e cristalli. Nessuna
immagine o filigrana è stata estratta dal video e riutilizzata nel sito.
Il PNG generato è stato convertito in JPEG con `sips` per la distribuzione
(1024 × 1536 pixel, circa 361 kB). Testi, busta, lembo, sigillo, icone e petali
restano elementi HTML/CSS/SVG distinti.

Prompt usato con il generatore integrato:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original decorative background for an elegant mobile-first Italian eighteenth birthday web invitation. Generate a tall portrait 2:3 image with luxurious photorealistic still-life craftsmanship. Composition: deep almost-black silk satin with subtle cobalt reflections, fine restrained gold chandelier and clear hanging faceted crystals descending from top center only in the top 22 percent; clusters of lush realistic deep blue and cobalt roses with dark leaves at lower left and lower right edges, a smaller blue rose partly entering upper right edge. Keep central 60 percent width and central vertical area empty almost black for live HTML typography and an animated envelope overlay. Far bottom soft blue haze. Roses not oversized: they frame but do not occupy the empty central area. Black 65 percent, midnight blue 25 percent, antique gold 10 percent. Warm small candle bulbs on chandelier, transparent crystal highlights with bluish edges, sophisticated low-key lighting and tactile satin folds. Show no phone, no screen, no envelope, no paper, no lettering, no numbers, no text, no border, no watermark, no logos, no red or pink objects, no confetti. The background will be cropped slightly on mobile; preserve large quiet negative space center. Crisp realistic roses and believable crystal material.

`images/favicon.svg` è un semplice monogramma creato nel progetto.
